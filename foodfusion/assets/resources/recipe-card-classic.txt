FoodFusion Recipe Card

Dish: Classic Tomato Pasta
Prep Time: 15 min
Cook Time: 25 min
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
- 400g pasta
- 2 cups crushed tomatoes
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper, basil

Steps:
1) Cook pasta until al dente.
2) Saut? garlic in olive oil, add tomatoes.
3) Simmer 10 minutes, season.
4) Toss with pasta and serve.
