About Jumanji

Nestled in the heart of an ancient wilderness, Jumanji Wildlife Park spans over 5,000 acres of diverse ecosystems. Our park is dedicated to conservation, education, and providing unforgettable encounters with some of the world's most magnificent creatures.

Founded in 1995, Jumanji has become a sanctuary for endangered species and a leader in wildlife preservation. Our expert guides will take you on a journey through savannahs, rainforests, and wetlands, where you'll witness animals in habitats that closely mirror their natural environments.

At Jumanji, we believe in the power of connection - between humans and the natural world, and between species and their ecosystems. Every visit supports our conservation efforts and helps protect these incredible animals for generations to come.

Meet Our Wildlife

African Lions

Observe our majestic pride of African lions in their expansive savannah habitat. These powerful predators are most active during our sunrise and sunset safari tours.

African Elephants

Witness the gentle giants of Jumanji as they roam our specially designed elephant plains. Our herd includes several multi-generational families.

Mountain Gorillas

Visit our misty mountain habitat where our family of critically endangered mountain gorillas resides. This immersive experience supports gorilla conservation worldwide.

Bengal Tigers

Marvel at the stunning beauty of our Bengal tigers in their forested enclosure. These elusive cats are part of an international breeding program for endangered species.

Unique Experiences

Safari Adventure

Join our expert guides on an open-air vehicle tour through our expansive savannah, where you'll encounter giraffes, zebras, antelopes, and predators in their naturalistic habitats.

Jungle Trek

Explore our rainforest habitat on foot with an experienced naturalist. Discover exotic birds, primates, and lush vegetation as you navigate elevated walkways through the canopy.

Night Safari

Experience the park after dark on our special night safari. Witness nocturnal behaviors of our animals and enjoy a traditional campfire dinner under the stars.

Keeper for a Day

Get behind-the-scenes access and assist our animal care team with daily routines. This exclusive program offers intimate encounters with some of our most popular residents.
